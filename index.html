✉️ headcse@uap-bd.edu 🔗 Profile

“Welcome to the Department of Computer Science and Engineering (CSE) at University of Asia Pacific (UAP), Bangladesh. The Department started its journey in 1996 by offering B.Sc. Engg. in CSE and later in 2006 added graduate programs. Our department is research-oriented, student-centered, and equipped with state-of-the-art programming, digital electronics, and industry-standard gaming labs.”

🎯 Department Vision

Striving for a pioneer role in ICT through excellence in education, research and development towards preparing graduates as global leaders with quality education, innovative ideas, and collaboration between industry and academia.

🚀 Key Facilities & Labs

  • 🖥️ 5 Dedicated Computer Programming Labs
  • 🔬 Final Year Student's Project & Thesis Lab
  • ⚡ Digital Electronics & Hardware Lab
  • 🎮 Industry-Standard Gaming Lab (ICT Division Supported)
  • 🎓 1500+ Enrolled Students • 39 Active Faculty Members
